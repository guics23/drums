Built with scripts/build_brushes.py (rebuild: python3 scripts/build_brushes.py <Swirly Samples dir> public/kits/brushes):
- 4 velocity layers per piece, taken at 40/60/80/100% of the source layers (the lowest ones are near-silent
  ghost strokes); 4 round robins each
- kick = marching kick (beater + reso mics), snare/edge = top + 0.6 × bottom mic
- clap track = brush stir (dynamic levels 1–4, main + 0.7 × skin mic, 3 s strokes from 0.5 s);
  stir_dl4_rr1 skipped (duplicate of its skin-mic file in the source)
- rim track = snare edge hits; toms: floor (low), 13" (mid), 10" (high)
- tails trimmed with fades: open hat 3 s, crash 4 s, ride 4.5 s
- one gain per piece (layers keep their relative dynamics), set so the 3rd layer (the app's normal
  velocity) is balanced across the kit and the loudest layer peaks at or below 0.95; 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono
Not included: cajon kick, buzz kick, snare dig/flutter, other hat articulations, 8"/12" toms, china,
splashes, broken crash, bongos, djembe, darbouka, and the "wet"/"wetter" room mic versions.
